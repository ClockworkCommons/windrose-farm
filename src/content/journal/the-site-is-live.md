Windrose.farm is live, which may seem early, since we haven't grown
anything yet.

We'd rather start now. If we waited for a harvest, the most useful part of
the record would be missing: the stretch where the ground is hard, the plans
are untested, and we don't know which ideas will work.

## What's here

- **[Our Story](/story/)**: where this came from, including Aunt Rosie, who
  kept bees from the age of sixteen.
- **[The Land](/land/)**: soil survey data, water, power, and the
  five-phase plan. This is the page with the uncomfortable numbers on it.
- **[The Farm](/farm/)**: what we plan to grow, and where each piece
  stands. So far every piece is a plan.
- **[The Seasons](/seasons/)**: photos of what the land does on its own
  through the year.
- **The journal**: this.

## What to expect

Not very much, very slowly, for a long time.

We'll write when something happens: a mound built, a swale cut, a tree
planted. Some of it will work and some of it won't. A farm blog where
everything succeeds is a farm blog that is leaving something out.

Nothing is for sale here. If honey or fruit ever comes of this in
quantities beyond what the family can eat, we'll mention it.

## Why bother

Partly to keep a record for ourselves. In ten years it will be useful to
know exactly how little we understood at the start.

Partly for folks looking at a hard piece of ground and wondering whether
it's worth the trouble. We don't know yet either.
